# Smart Line Management System (SLMS) - YourTera

## Overview

<img width="1920" height="1058" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e3128599-f213-4393-b779-65cec2238980" />


Smart Line Management System (SLMS), also called **YourTera**, is a web-based queue management system designed to reduce long waiting times in public service offices such as:

- Revenue Offices
- Telecom Branches
- Kebele Administrations
- Government institutions

Instead of standing in physical lines, users can join queues online, receive a digital ticket, track their queue position, and get notified when their turn is approaching.

The system helps improve service efficiency and provides a smoother experience for both citizens and staff.

---

## What the System Does

### For Users

Users can:

- Register and log into the system
- Select an office and required service
- Join a virtual queue
- Receive a digital ticket
- View their queue position
- See estimated waiting time
- Receive notifications when their turn is near
- View required documents for selected services

### For Administrators

Administrators can:

- Manage queues
- Manage services and offices
- Update service requirements
- Monitor users and queue activity
- View reports and analytics

---

## Main Goal

The goal of the system is to:

- Reduce long waiting lines
- Save users time
- Improve customer experience
- Digitize queue management in public institutions
